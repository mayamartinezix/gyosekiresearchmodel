# Photo log (ground data) - simple rules
Goal: same places, same way, every time, so changes mean something.
Time budget: 15-20 minutes a week total. Stop if it takes more.

## Rules for all companies
- Pick 2-3 fixed places per company. Never change them.
- Same day and time each visit (example: Saturday 12:30).
- Photograph things anyone can see. Don't photograph people's faces.
- Many Japanese stores limit photos. Ask a staff member first, or skip that place.
- Log every photo in `photo_log.csv` the same day (date, company, place, what, number).
- No asking staff for private information (sales numbers, plans).

## Skylark (easy)
Pick 2 restaurants (for example Gusto and Bamiyan).
- Menu price of 5 fixed items. Photograph the menu monthly. (Price rises = a signal.)
- Waiting line / empty seats at lunch peak. Count the people waiting.
- Promotions posted (campaigns, limited menus).
- Closures, new stores, conversions to another brand near you.

## Sapporo (medium)
Pick 1 supermarket and 1 convenience store.
- Beer shelf: count how many shelf slots Sapporo/Yebisu has vs rivals (Asahi, Kirin, Suntory).
- Price tags for 3 fixed products.
- Promotion displays and end-of-aisle stacks.
- Pubs/izakaya: is Sapporo or a rival on draft?

## Sumitomo Forestry (harder)
- Japan: one housing exhibition park (住宅展示場). Note crowding, promotions, and how busy Sumitomo Forestry's model home is vs rivals. Photos may be limited, so notes are fine.
- Construction sites with Sumitomo Forestry signs near you (count per month).
- US: your family photographs 2 new-home communities every 2 weeks (see earlier plan).

## Backtest use
A photo log starts today, so it has NO history. It can't be backtested on the past.
Use it as a forward check: do the readings agree with your model's direction? Say this clearly in your report.
