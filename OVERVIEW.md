## Sumitomo Forestry (1911) - housing and wood, your main company
**Standing**
- Sales ¥2.27T (FY25); FY26 forecast ¥2.95T because of the Tri Pointe purchase.
- FY26 profit is forecast down sharply: recurring income 115.0 (FY25: 174.9), net income 60.0 (FY25: 106.7). Cut on Aug 7.
- Overseas Housing is about 79% of profit. US profit margin falls 13.4% -> 8.2%.
- Debt doubled to ¥1.6T after the Tri Pointe purchase. Goodwill write-offs and interest cost about ¥30B a year.
- Japan housing orders up 14 months in a row; US orders up but with heavier discounts.
**Can affect it**
- US mortgage rates (7.40% now), US home prices and incentives.
- Yen level (USD/JPY 158; weaker yen raises reported profit).
- US lumber tariffs and prices, sawmill start-up.
- Japan material costs, Australia affordability.
**Test these**
1. US mortgage rate and builder incentives vs US housing margin.
2. Japan monthly orders/backlog vs next quarters' sales.

## Skylark (3197) - family restaurants
**Standing**
- Sales ¥458B (FY25) -> ¥500B guided; operating profit 30.0 -> 35.0 guided.
- Growing in all three ways: customers, prices, acquisitions.
- Raised guidance in Aug 2026; beat its own raised guide last year.
- Margins are exposed to wages and food prices.
**Can affect it**
- Wage rises (October minimum-wage increase), food and energy costs.
- Price rises accepted or rejected by customers.
- Weather, tourism, consumer mood.
- Acquisition integration and debt.
**Test these**
1. The company's monthly same-store sales and customer counts vs quarterly operating profit.
2. Wage and food cost indices vs profit margin.
