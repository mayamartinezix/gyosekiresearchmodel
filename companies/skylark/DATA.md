# Skylark Holdings (3197) - Public Data
Gathered Oct 17, 2026. ¥ million unless noted. Dec fiscal year. **IFRS** (so "business profit" exists: operating profit before one-off items).
Source quality: H1 FY26 and FY25 numbers come from the Aug 13, 2026 results PDF (read through a summarizer). Q1 and older items come from third-party summaries. **Verify key numbers in the English IR pages before using.**

## Standing in 8 bullets
- Japan's biggest family-restaurant group: Gusto, Bamiyan, Shabu-yo, Jonathan's, Yumean and others. Stores: 3,214 (Jun 30, 2026).
- Growing fast: H1 sales +9.7%, operating profit +20.9%, net income +29.2%.
- Growth comes from three places: more customers, higher prices, and buying other chains (Shinpachi Shokudo, about ¥11B, Apr 2026; Sukesan Udon).
- Same-store sales (existing stores) H1: +6.5% (customers +1.6%, average spend +4.8%).
- Guidance was raised on Aug 13, 2026 (operating profit 33.5B -> 35.0B).
- Track record: it has under-promised. FY25 operating profit came in at 30.0B vs about 25.0B guided before the Nov 2025 raise (see 1.3).
- Costs to watch: wages (Q1 staff costs +¥3.3B), food costs, utilities, borrowing costs for acquisitions.
- Only one reporting segment (restaurants), so no segment profit split to model.

## 2. Things that can move it (outside)
- **Wages:** Japan minimum wage rises each October; labor shortage.
- **Food and ingredient costs:** rice, beef, cooking oil, imports (yen).
- **Prices:** how many price rises customers accept (average spend is +4.8%).
- **Tourists and consumer mood:** inbound tourism, real wage growth.
- **Weather:** heat, typhoons, snow reduce visits.
- **Acquisitions:** integration cost, extra debt.
- **Debt:** one outlet's headline mentions debt concerns after the acquisitions (article not read; check the balance sheet).
