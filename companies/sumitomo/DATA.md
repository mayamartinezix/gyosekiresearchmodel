### 1.7 Timber, Real Estate, Environment [BR][Q&A]
- Timber: forecast cut by ¥5.0 billion because the US sawmill (Plain Dealing) ramps up late. It loses money at the EBITDA level now; expected to turn profitable in Q4 FY26. Management: timber product prices "stable."
- Real Estate: US property sales plan cut from 20 to 10 (10 pushed to next year); valuation losses on some LP investments. Combined effect about ¥16.5 billion. US real estate loss forecast -12.5 billion yen. Management does not expect a big US real estate recovery next year.
- Environment & Resources: 1H sales 13.8 billion yen (+9.1%), recurring income -1.2 billion yen.
- Japan wood plant (Kowanomori Yotsukura): about 110,000 m3 logs per year. 2030 target: 1 million m3 of Japanese timber used; 1 million ha of forest owned/managed.
- Forest/carbon spending: NOT disclosed in what I read.

### 1.8 Tri Pointe (TPH) [Q&A][BR]
- Price/funds: about ¥610 billion. Bridge loan 610; hybrid financing about 350 (including subordinated bonds about 250); debt financing about 260. Share sales about ¥50 billion planned.
- Goodwill: about ¥94 billion, written off over 10 years = about ¥10 billion per year (provisional).
- Interest: about ¥17 billion per year.
- Fair-value adjustment write-off: about ¥19.7 billion this year, about ¥5 billion next year.
- One-time costs: about ¥2 billion next year (moving to permanent financing).
- TPH own profit: ¥30 billion recurring income for Apr-Dec 2026; 3,870 units planned. Next year's outlook due Feb 2027.
- TPH in Q2: sales about ¥111 billion, recurring income about ¥5.4 billion, about 1,013 units (calculated by differencing).
- "Others & adjustments" recurring income: FY26F -42.5 billion yen at that line (vs +6.2 in FY25) [BR].
- Interest-bearing debt: 769.5 -> 1,599.0 billion yen (+829.5). Net debt Jun-26 about 1,351.4 billion yen.
- Consolidation date unclear (closed May 14; notes say Apr 1 - Jun 30). Ask or check the filing.
